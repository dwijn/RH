title: Passive atoms freed by Background() no longer leak their arrays; Kurucz level labels no longer leak or overflow; CI runs the end-to-end tests with the sanitizers

LeakSanitizer reported leaks at the end of the 2-D runs (`eddington_2d` and `solveray_cosines` failed with the sanitizers):
- `Background()` frees the passive atoms when there is no hydrostatic equilibrium and `SOLVE_NE` is below `ITERATION`, as in the 2-D template. `freeAtomicLine` freed a line's `c_shift` and `c_fraction` only in an active atom. `freeAtomicContinuum` never freed a continuum's `lambda`, nor the `alpha` of a hydrogenic continuum. That lost 54 kB in 539 allocations with the template's atoms.
- `RLKdet_level` (`kurucz.c`) did not free the word array of `getWords` when it could not parse a Kurucz level label: one allocation per such line.

CI did not see them: its sanitizer job ran only the unit tests, and `run_tests.py` turns leak detection off.

**The fix.**
- `freeAtomicLine` frees the components of every line.
- `freeAtomicContinuum` frees `alpha` always and `lambda` in a passive atom. In an active atom `lambda` points into the spectrum (`SortLambda`).
- `RLKdet_level` frees the word array on every return.
- `getWords` (`zeeman.c`) allocated room for `length / 2 + 1` pointers. That is one too few for the end marker when the label has the most words its length allows ("a b c"), and for an empty label. It now allocates `length / 2 + 2` and stops at once on an empty label. Found by the new unit test; Kurucz labels (10 characters) never reach that limit.
- CI's sanitizer job also runs the end-to-end tests (`ctest -L e2e`, leak detection on). Locally that takes about 5 minutes with 4 jobs.

**Results.** Unchanged: `run_tests.py --exact` against `modernize` 90d5ffd.

Tests: new unit test `test_rlklabel`.
- It parses a Kurucz label in LS coupling.
- Two unparsable labels return FALSE.
- `getWords` finds every word of "a b c" and "a".

Under the sanitizers it fails without the fix to `RLKdet_level` (LeakSanitizer: 32 bytes in 2 allocations). The 2-D end-to-end tests pass with the sanitizers.
