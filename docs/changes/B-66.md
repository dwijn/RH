title: Bézier diagonal operator no larger than the weight of S: no negative preconditioned upward rates

The scalar cubic Bézier solvers (1-D, 2-D, 3-D) set the diagonal operator to Ψ = β + θ. One Bézier step gives

I_loc = ε I_uw + α S_uw + β S_loc + γ C_uw + θ C_loc,

with C_loc = S_loc − Δτ/3 S′_loc. The weights are positive, and the Steffen-limited derivatives keep both control points between the values they join. So with S ≥ 0

I_loc − (β + θ) S_loc = ε I_uw + α S_uw + γ C_uw + θ (C_loc − S_loc),

and only the last term can be negative. Where S rises along the ray, C_loc < S_loc; at the last point of a ray, whose derivative is one-sided, C_loc = (2 S_loc + S_uw)/3. β + θ takes C_loc to move one for one with S_loc, so there it exceeds the weight of S_loc in I_loc. Then I − Ψη in `addPreconditionedRates` (`fillgamma.c`) is negative, and with it the upward radiative rate of that wavelength and ray.

The negative contributions are common. On two columns of a MURaM quiet-Sun snapshot (5 G run, t = 1800 s, H_6 active from ZERO_RADIATION, 128 and 322 points), 8.5 M of 278 M and 9.3 M of 414 M per-wavelength, per-ray upward contributions were negative. Their sums usually stay positive, which is why the N-8 limiter was left out ("a failure that does not occur"). It does occur on a third column, started from LTE populations. Its top point is at 19.8 kK above a point at 6.8 kK, so S of Ly α jumps by orders of magnitude across one optically thick cell. In the first iteration the summed H 1s → 2p rate at k = 0 is −1.0×10⁵ s⁻¹, the rate equations fall back on LU, n₁ comes out negative (−7.0×10⁹), and the run stops. With `S_INTERPOLATION = S_LINEAR`, whose other weights are all non-negative, the same case has no negative contribution and no LU fallback.

`Bezier3_psi` (`bezier_aux.c`) now gives Ψ = β + θ min(1, C_loc/S_loc). It counts θ C_loc as part of the weight of S_loc only as far as C_loc ≤ S_loc, which gives I_loc ≥ Ψ S_loc on every ray, and it is β + θ wherever S does not rise along the ray. It is used by `Piecewise_Bezier3_1D`, `_2D` and `_3D`. Ψ is the preconditioner only, so the converged populations do not change.

This is not the exact diagonal of N-13 (closed as not planned). That one is larger than β + θ, made I − Ψη cancel more in thick cores, and converged slower. This Ψ is smaller. Nor is it the N-8 limiter in `addPreconditionedRates`, which acted on every run's rates; this one changes Ψ only where C_loc < S_loc, in the formal solution. The polarized DELO-Bézier step (`Bezier3_Stokes_step`) keeps β + θ: its control points are those of S_eff = S − K′I, and B-55 covers that case.

Measured with H_6 active on the three MURaM columns (Anderson):
- **The LTE-start column:** no negative contribution in 200 iterations (70 105 before the stop on the base). The run no longer stops, but it does not converge either: that stall is a separate problem (period-2 cycle of Anderson; a slow mode of the plain iteration in cool, low-nₑ layers).
- **The two columns that converge from ZERO_RADIATION:** 147 and 79 iterations to 10⁻³ (160 and 79 on the base). To 10⁻⁸ they take 210 and 154 iterations (223 and 157), and the populations agree with the base to 5×10⁻⁷ and the spectra to 1.8×10⁻⁷. To 10⁻⁵ they agreed to 6×10⁻⁴: the difference is the convergence error, and the fixed point is the same.
- **The rare negative downward contributions** (0.06 %, bound B of the N-8 note) are unchanged: they do not come from Ψ.

Cost, `run_stress.py --set conv` with `ITER_LIMIT=1e-6;N_MAX_ITER=400` (all 1-D cases, `2d_pert_h6_ca_mg`, `3d_pert_h6_ca`; the Bifrost cases not run), base vs this change:
- **18 cases that both builds converge:** formal solutions 0.98× the base (geometric mean). The range is 0.65× (`1d_shock_h6`, 385 → 250 iterations) to 1.09× (`1d_falc_fe33`, 28 → 31). `1d_falc_h6_ca_mg` goes 40 → 43, 2-D 82 → 79, 3-D 83 → 78. LU fallbacks are unchanged (0, and 1 in `shock_h6`).
- **Two Ng cases that the base does not converge in 400 iterations** (`1d_falc_nong`, `1d_falc_h6_tight`, oscillating at 3.8×10⁻³ and 2.9×10⁻³) now converge in 183 and 158.
- **`1d_falc_prdng`** (Ng on ρ, `PRD_ITER_LIMIT = 1e-2`) takes 288 iterations instead of 29, and the two results differ by 0.14 in I. Neither build converges that case: with `ITER_LIMIT` and `PRD_ITER_LIMIT` at 10⁻⁹, both run all 2000 iterations (PRD change 2.5×10⁻⁵ on the base, 4.8×10⁻⁴ here), and the base's 29-iteration result is 0.06 from its own 2000-iteration one.
- **`1d_falxco_collrel`** converges on neither (400 iterations on both).
