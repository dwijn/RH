title: Only CO can be active: other molecules stop at input instead of getting CO's collisional rates

The collisional rates of an active molecule were chosen by substring: an ID containing "CO" got `COcollisions`, one containing "H2" (H₂, but also H2O and H2+) got `H2collisions`, and anything else stopped at the setup of the first iteration. `h2collisions.c` was `cocollisions.c` with only the names changed: CO's vibrational constant (2103 cm⁻¹; H₂'s fundamental is about 4161 cm⁻¹) and CO's Landau–Teller constants, with the CO–H₂ term turned into a term in the CO density. An active H₂ with a line list ran with those rates.

**The fix.**
- `readMolecule` stops at input for an active molecule whose ID is not exactly `CO`. The message names the molecule and says to make it `PASSIVE`.
- `h2collisions.c` is removed, and `initSolution` calls `COcollisions` directly.

No shipped input changes. Of the shipped molecule files, only `CO_NLTE` and `TiO` have a line list. An active TiO stopped at the setup of the first iteration and now stops at input. An active H₂ or H2O (no line list) stopped with "No lines specified" and now stops with the new message.

**CO checked against the paper.** `cocollisions.c` matches Ayres & Wiedemann (1989, ApJ 338, 1033):
- the Landau–Teller form, their eqs. (18) and (19);
- the constants, their Table 2 (H and H₂ from Glass & Kironde 1982, He from Millikan 1964);
- the electron rate, the formula of their Appendix IV (after Thompson 1973, given for low temperatures);
- `OMEGA0` = 2103 cm⁻¹, the harmonic constant of their eq. (3). With the rigid rotor it reproduces the term values for v < 4, so it is not CO's 1–0 band centre (2143 cm⁻¹), and it is not a typo.

The header of `cocollisions.c` now says so. Its values are unchanged.

**Not done here** (wishlist): collision data in the molecule file instead of the code, and rates for other molecules. For H₂ with H, Lique (2015, MNRAS 453, 810) gives state-to-state ro-vibrational rate coefficients for 100–5000 K, in the paper's supplementary data. RH's model (vibrational levels, rotational levels in LTE, one v-independent rate) would need them averaged over j. H₂ also needs rates with H₂, He and electrons, and a quadrupole line list.

**Results.** Unchanged: `run_tests.py --exact` against `modernize` a34785a.

Tests: new end-to-end test `active_molecules`.
- An active H₂ with a line list stops at input; before, it ran with CO's rates.
- An active H2O stops with the new message.
- An active CO with the v ≤ 3 line list runs and writes its populations. No other test made a molecule active.
