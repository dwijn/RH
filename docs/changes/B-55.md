title: Polarized DELO steps fall back to an exponential step where they would amplify the Stokes vector

With `STOKES_MODE = FULL_STOKES` the 2-D and 3-D iterations diverged on the small test atmospheres with a 500 G field that varies across the columns (and died with SIGFPE in upstream's default configuration, `DELO_BEZIER3`). Cause: the DELO steps treat K′I as part of a source function interpolated over the step. Where the magneto-optical terms are large over an optically long step and fall steeply across it (ρ_V 5–60 times χ_I in the Ca II wings, dropping about 10 times between columns), the upwind control point extrapolates K′²·I_uw with nothing to cancel it, and the step amplifies the polarization: over random physical K′ the Bézier step's worst amplification of |I| (S = 0, where the exact solution cannot grow) is 0.986 for max|K′|Δτ ≤ 1, 1.4 at 3 and up to 1.4×10⁴ beyond; DELO-linear (the polarized long characteristics, N-41) is at most 0.998 up to 0.25 and up to 8 beyond. A 1-D field rarely does this (its K′ varies smoothly with depth), which is why 1-D converged.

`Stokes_Exp_step` (`bezier_aux.c`) now takes over in `Bezier3_Stokes_step` where max|K′|Δτ > 1 and in `LongChar_Stokes_Linear` where it exceeds 0.2, and in both only where |η| ≤ 1 at both ends (|η| > 1 means a Zeeman component with negative opacity; the transfer equation itself amplifies there, and the DELO step is kept as before). It integrates dI/dτ = S − (1 + K′)I exactly over two half steps, K′ = K′_uw on the first and K′_loc on the second, with S the same cubic Hermite polynomial the Bézier step uses, split at the midpoint: e^A, φ₁ … φ₄ of each 4 × 4 A from a Taylor series of the scaled matrix and structured doubling (`exp_phi4`; a thick half, Δτ(1 − |η|) > 40, asymptotically with one inverse). It never increases the norm of the Stokes vector, is exact for constant K′ and cubic S, and gives the local equilibrium in a thick step. A first version held K′ at the mean of the two ends; in thick steps that left I up to 5 % below the local source where the local dichroism exceeded the upwind one, and the nearly cancelled preconditioned rates of the Ca II H core turned negative (found by an independent analysis; the same in 1-D with an inclination alternating between 15° and 75°). The approximate operator stays the scalar β + θ of the Bézier step: the exponential step's own coefficient of S_I in I, (K⁻¹)₀₀ in a thick layer, reaches 1.19 with dichroism, and with it the first population update inverted Ca II 866 nm.

Results:
- 2-D, `FULL_STOKES`, varying field: converges like the uniform field and like B = 0 (PRD sub-iteration deltas 0.278, 0.115, 0.061, 0.027 against 0.280, 0.112, 0.060, 0.027 for B = 0); before, J changed by 10³–10⁶ and the run stopped on a negative opacity. 3-D (4 × 4 columns, the same field pattern): converges likewise (0.258, 0.112, 0.063, 0.028); before, the PRD delta reached 1.8×10⁷ and the run stopped on a negative opacity in iteration 1.
- Cost: on that case the exponential step replaces 28 % of the Bézier steps (5.6 of 20 million) and 29 % of the long-characteristic sections; the 2-D run with the uniform field takes 74 s of CPU time against 26 s before (the varying one 72 s; it did not converge before). The 4 × 4 algorithm is 20 times faster than the 20 × 20 block exponential it replaced in development.
- `test_stokes_step` (new): closed forms (K′ = 0 with cubic S to 2×10⁻¹⁵; Faraday rotation to 5×10⁻¹² per 10⁴ of ρΔτ), cubic exactness (one step against two halves, 6×10⁻¹⁵), the thick limit (3×10⁻⁵ at Δτ = 10⁴), and no amplification of any of the three steps over 10⁵ random physical steps. `run_tests.sh` gets `2d/fullstokes`.
