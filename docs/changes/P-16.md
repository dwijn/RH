title: Hybrid PRD: `Opacity` no longer allocates a wavelength-sized scratch array on every call

With `PRD_ANGLE_DEP = PRD_ANGLE_APPROX`, `Opacity` (`opacity.c`) `calloc`ed `rho_tmp`, Nspect doubles, at the start of every call and freed it at the end. Only the `PRDH_LIMIT_MEM` branch, which interpolates ρ on the fly, uses it. In a moving atmosphere `Opacity` runs for every wavelength, ray and direction: with 118 501 wavelengths that is about 1.2 million allocations of 0.95 MB per sweep, each of which the allocator hands back with `madvise` and refills with page faults. It is now allocated only with `PRDH_LIMIT_MEM`.

1-D Bifrost lane column (77 points, Fe I+II and Mg active, 118 501 wavelengths, moving, hybrid PRD), Apple M2 Pro: a sample of all threads during the iterations put 10 % of the worker samples in `madvise` under this `calloc`. Wall time per iteration and system time, before → after: 1 thread 47.0 → 42.0 s, 35 → 14 s; 2 threads 27.3 → 23.9 s, 53 → 20 s; 4 threads 17.3 → 14.7 s, 76 → 33 s.

Results do not change: on one thread the populations, J, `Jgas` and the flux are identical to the bit after three iterations.
