title: LTE Saha chains (LTEpops_elem) and Saha-Boltzmann populations of atoms (LTEpops) computed in logarithms, relative to the dominant term

`LTEpops_elem` built the ionization balance as a chain of linear Saha factors relative to the neutral stage, `n_i/n_0 = Π CT_ne exp(U_{i+1} − U_i − χ_i/kT)`, and normalized by their sum. For an element with many stages the products overflow where the ionization is high (the first to go is a stage at low `ne`, where each `CT_ne` is large): `inf` divided by `inf` gave NaN populations of every stage. `LTEpops` did the same for the levels of an atom (`n*` relative to level 0, `exp(−ΔE/kT)/(CT_ne)^ΔZ`).

Both now compute the logarithm of each term (`ln CT_ne + ln U_{i+1} − ln U_i − χ_i/kT` per stage; `ln g_i/g_0 − ΔE/kT − ΔZ ln CT_ne` per level), subtract the largest and exponentiate, so the dominant stage (level) is exact and a minor one underflows only on its own. The clip of `n*` at `DBL_MIN` stays as a guard against exact zeros (ratios of `n*` in the collision rates, `ZERO_RADIATION` in `initial_xdr.c` and the opacities divide by it); `LTEpops_elem` has no clip. Results for physical parameters change at the rounding level only (1e-13 relative).

`tests/unit/test_ltepops.c`: element with the C I-VII ionization potentials and a synthetic 30-stage one, an atom of 4 stages with 3 levels each, 25 temperatures 3000 K to 10⁶ K × 18 electron densities 10⁻¹⁰ to 10²⁴ m⁻³, against a linear long double reference: populations finite, non-negative, summing to the total; dominant stages (above 10⁻³ of the total) agree to 3×10⁻¹³ (C I-VII 5×10⁻¹⁴, atom 3×10⁻¹⁴), minor ones to 10⁻¹² + 10⁻¹⁴ |ln(n/total)| down to 10⁻²⁸⁰ of the total. The old double chain gives NaN at 286 of 450 points of the 30-stage sweep (e.g. T = 3822 K, ne = 10⁻¹⁰ m⁻³, where stage 16 holds 1.2×10¹³ times the total), and is right at all 450 points for C I-VII.
