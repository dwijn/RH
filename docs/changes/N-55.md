ids: N-55
date: 2026-11-21
title: A symmetric line whose blue half contains wavelengths of other transitions gets a full profile (blends)

An active line without `ASYMM` in a static, unpolarized atmosphere with one component is symmetric: its wavelength grid covers only the red half, and it is entered in the active set only at λ ≥ λ₀ (`SortLambda`). Another transition with wavelength points in the blue half of such a line saw none of its opacity there. Nothing warned about it.

**The fix (Option A, user, 2026-11-20).** `asymmetricBlends` (`getlambda.c`), called by `SortLambda` before the wavelengths are collected, rebuilds a symmetric line of an active atom as asymmetric when its blue half, [2λ₀ − λ_last, λ₀), contains a wavelength of:
- another active transition: a line, a continuum point (points beyond the edge count at the edge, as `SortLambda` puts them), or a line of an active molecule;
- the wavelength table;
- the reference wavelength.

The rebuild doubles `Nlambda` and calls `getLambda`, so the red half is unchanged, bit for bit, and the blue half is its mirror image. The check repeats until nothing changes, because a line made asymmetric can put points in the blue half of another one. Each line changed prints one message (" -- Line j -> i of atom X (λ₀ nm): other transitions have wavelengths in its blue half; full profile (N-55)").

**Measured** (FAL-C, static, ITER_LIMIT 10⁻⁶, against `modernize` 77e50f7; evidence `claude/RH/evidence/t28-n55`):
- H_20 + Ca II: 13 lines made asymmetric, 4276 → 4536 wavelengths (+6 %), wall time +7 %. Emergent I changes by 10 % in Ca II H (the Hε blend), 3.5 % at Pa16 (850 nm), 0.8 % at Pa13 (866 nm). Populations change by at most 2.5×10⁻³ (H) and 2.1×10⁻³ (Ca).
- H_CRD + Ca II: 1 line, +2 % wavelengths, I up to 0.7 % at Ca II H/Hε.
- H_6 + Ca II, H_6 + Ca II + Mg II, Ca II alone: no line changes, byte-identical results.

**Not fixed here (N-62).** A symmetric line whose blue half has no points still has no points there, so every other transition whose wavelength range spans that blue half integrates J across it with the trapezoid from line centre to the next point blueward. With H_20 that changes the Ca II populations by up to 40 % in the upper chromosphere (the Ca II continua across the Lyman lines). That is a quadrature error, not a missing opacity, and it is logged as N-62.

**Results.** Unchanged for the regression suite: `run_tests.py` against `modernize` b115440 (tolerance mode), 38 of 38 pass and every comparison with the baseline is exactly 0 (no suite case has wavelengths in the blue half of a symmetric line). Models with such blends change, as measured above.

Tests: new unit test `test_blends`. It checks which lines are made asymmetric (a continuum point, another line's red half, a table wavelength; not a passive atom's line, nor a wavelength at line centre). It also checks that the chain needs a second pass, that the red half is unchanged bit for bit with the blue half its mirror, and that a second call changes nothing.
