title: Anderson's trial-point guard no longer tests line inversions

The guard (`guardTrial`) rejected or damped an extrapolated point that inverted a line (n_l/g_l > n_u/g_u) at a depth where G's point did not. It was meant to keep a strong inverted line from making the total opacity negative at its centre, where the formal solver overflows (SOLVER.md trap 5). It does not do that: the total opacity goes negative at G's own points (the ZERO_RADIATION starting solutions of `falc_helarge`, `falc_civi` and `falc_mgi66`, down to −44 χ_c on `falc_helarge`) and at `falc_helarge`'s converged solution (504 points, down to −6.6 χ_c, IR He I 4296 nm), and the deepest run of negative slant optical depth, −0.094 on `falc_mgi66`, occurs with the test on (−0.026 without). Converged solutions also have inverted lines (S-13). The guard keeps the finite and negative-value checks; a negative total opacity deep enough to overflow is now detected where it happens, in the formal solution (N-40). Newton's log line no longer counts inversion halvings. Decided by the user 2026-11-13.
