title: solve1d, solve2d, formal2d and formal3d retired

Four stand-alone programs are gone, with their IDL drivers: `solve1d` and `solve2d` (with `iter_1d.c` and `iter_2d.c`) solved a two-level problem with their own `Iterate`, from a binary parameter stream on stdin or a `2dinput.dat` file; `formal2d` and `formal3d` did one formal solution from a binary stream on stdin and wrote I and Ψ to stdout. None had tests, none used RH's input files, and none fits the library design, in which one `rh` program and the library serve every use (`docs/LIBRARY.md` §7, decided 2026-11-24; full backward compatibility is not a goal).

Removed: `rhf1d/solve1d.c`, `rhf1d/iter_1d.c`, `rhsc2d/solve2d.c`, `rhsc2d/iter_2d.c`, `rhsc2d/formal2d.c`, `rhsc3d/formal3d.c`, their targets, and `idl/solve1d.pro`, `idl/solve2d.pro`, `idl/formal2d.pro`, `idl/formal3d.pro`. The results of the other programs do not change.
