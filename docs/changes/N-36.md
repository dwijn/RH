title: Chemical equilibrium of the legacy iteration takes the neutral fraction of active atoms from their populations

`chemicalEquilibrium()` (`chemequil.c`) needs the neutral fraction `fn0` of every nucleus: it enters the H- density (`fHmin = ne fn0 PhiHmin`), the molecular Saha equations and so `ntotal`, the molecular densities and ne. For an active atom the map G of the G solvers (`ChemicalEquilibriumOfState`, S-2b) took it from the current populations, normalized by their sum. The legacy path (`ChemicalEquilibrium`, SOLVER = NG with SOLVE_NE = ITERATION, and the Background() call at setup) took it from the LTE `nstar` unless the run started from OLD_POPULATIONS. The two therefore converged to fixed points of different models, and the legacy result depended on how the run was started. Now both take it from `atom->n` divided by the sum of the populations. The sum equals `ntotal` after every call (the populations are rescaled by n_atomic/ntotal in place and statistical equilibrium conserves `ntotal`), so the normalization change alone does not move results; passive atoms are as before. `ChemicalEquilibriumOfState` is not changed in behaviour.

Cause found by running `1d_falc_solvene` (FAL-C, H_6 + Ca II, PRD, BACKGROUND_DOUBLE, ITER_LIMIT 1e-10, no acceleration: NG_ORDER = 0, PRD_NG_ORDER = 0, PRD_ITER_LIMIT = 0, N_MAX_SCATTER = 50; all 466 iterations, delta 9.8e-11) as A = SOLVER = NG and B = SOLVER = FIXED_POINT on 936aa39. A against B: populations 4.85e-6, spectrum 1.70e-4 of the peak at 163.2 nm (worst wavelength), lines 4.68e-6; ne up to 1.4e-6, nH 4.6e-6, and H2, CH, NH, OH densities differ by a factor of up to 7e4 at T ~ 9000 K (they are (fn0 n)^2). Experiments with only the `fn0` source switched: C = B with the LTE fraction against A: populations 1.7e-11, spectrum 1.0e-11; D = A with the populations against B: populations 1.7e-10, spectrum 9.8e-9, lines 4.3e-9 (the 1e-10 level of convergence). So `fn0` is the whole difference. The populations are the consistent choice: `Solve_ne` already uses the NLTE H populations, and with LTE fractions a restart (OLD_POPULATIONS) changed the problem.

Measured with this change (candidate build against the 936aa39 build):
- `1d_falc_solvene`, NG, no acceleration recipe above: 466 iterations, delta 9.77e-11 as before; the result moves by 4.85e-6 (populations), 1.70e-4 of the peak (spectrum), 4.7e-6 (lines), and now agrees with FIXED_POINT to 1.7e-10 / 9.8e-9 / 4.3e-9. With NG_ORDER 2 (NG_DELAY 10, NG_PERIOD 3) and ITER_LIMIT 1e-6: 69 iterations in both.
- SOLVER = ANDERSON (default) on `1d_falc_solvene` and `1d_shock_h6crd_ne`, ITER_LIMIT 1e-10: iterations 66 and 70 as before; populations differ by 2.2e-10 and 2.4e-10, spectrum by 9.8e-9 and 3.5e-9, which is the noise at the convergence limit (SOLVER.md 3.7); the setup Background() now sees the starting populations (see below).
- `1d_shock_h6crd_ne` with SOLVER = NG does not work before or after: the iteration oscillates with period 2 (N-6; 600 iterations delta 1.4 with Ng, 3000 iterations delta 246 without). With the change it stops earlier with `badPopulations` (negative LU populations at k = 48) at iteration 10 (no acceleration) and 587 (with Ng). Use a G solver with SOLVE_NE = ITERATION.

Which other results change: the setup `Background()` also calls the legacy path, with the starting populations instead of the LTE ones. Where an active atom does not start in LTE (the H populations of FALC_82.atmos), the first chemical equilibrium differs: `run_tests.sh` cases `1d/twoactive` (2.0e-4 at 163.16 nm) and `sph/small` (3.0e-4) change, in 4 iterations of SOLVE_NE = NONE/ONCE runs. All other cases are byte-identical at N = 1. Label: changes-results.

`ctest` and `clang-format` as in the report of the branch; no new test: the check is the A/B comparison above (a regression test would need a 466-iteration run).
