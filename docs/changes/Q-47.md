title: Dead PII() and substring() removed; the tables nothing writes are const (results unchanged)

`PII()` (`giigen.c`, the angle-averaged redistribution function by Gauss quadrature, whose lazy initialization was unguarded) and `substring()` (`getline.c`) had no callers; they and their prototypes are gone. `GII()`, the approximation that the PRD code uses, stays.

Every initialized static of the library and the programs that nothing writes is now `const`. These are the 13 tables of the log row: the H₂⁻ and H₂⁺ free-free tables (`hydrogen.c`), the OH and CH bound-free tables (`ohchbf.c`) and `atomweight` (`atomweights.h`). Listing the writable initialized data of an -O0 build (`nm`) found 14 more: the H₂ Rayleigh table and its long-wavelength coefficients (`hydrogen.c`), the coefficients of `E1` and of the gamma function, `getCPU`'s clock scale, the Gauss–Laguerre nodes and weights of `tools/laguerre.c`, and the name tables of `solver.c` and `work.c`, whose pointers are now `const` too. The survey of phase 2 had missed them: gcc already keeps unwritten statics in read-only data at -O2, and the survey left out read-only symbols. The only writable initialized static left in the library is the lazy-initialization flag of the Wigner symbols (phase 4).

`Hunt`, `Locate` and `bilinear` take `const double *`. `bilinear` was declared separately in `hydrogen.c` and `ohchbf.c`; its prototype is now in `rh.h`, next to `Hunt`.

Machine code at -O0, where gcc translates each expression as written: 357 of 360 object files (library, programs, tools, unit tests) are identical to the base, apart from the tables now being in read-only sections. The other three are `getline.c` and `giigen.c`, which only lose `substring` and `PII`, and `hydrogen.c`. There gcc reads an element of a `const` table at a constant index as a constant in the literal pool, even at -O0: 4 loads from `thetaFF_H2m` and `tempFF_H2p` become literal loads, and 10 other literal loads move. Each of the 14 reads the same 8 bytes as before. The bytes of all tables are unchanged. Scripts and logs: `claude/RH/evidence/lib-q47-const`.

Part of Q-4; library track (`docs/LIBRARY.md` §6.3). Results unchanged: `run_tests.py --exact` against the base, all geometries.
