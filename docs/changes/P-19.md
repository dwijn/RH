title: The exponential Stokes step of B-55 in closed form: a 2-D FULL_STOKES run back to the cost before B-55

B-55 (#371) made the polarized DELO steps fall back to an exact exponential step where max |K'| Δτ is large. Each half step evaluates e^A and φ₁…φ₄ of A = −Δτ (1 + K′), a 4 × 4 matrix, by a Taylor series and squarings (`exp_phi4`). That doubled the cost of a 2-D `FULL_STOKES` run: on the `run_tests` case (FALC_03x82 with the field of `make_field.py`, Ca II, one thread, four iterations) `exp_phi4` took 60 % of the time, 3.8 µs per call over 10.3 M calls, 1.85 s of the 3.2 s per formal solution (1.36 s before B-55, measured on the commit before #371 for the formal solutions it completed before diverging). The fallback is taken in 32 % of the Bézier steps there. Most of its calls are not thick steps but steps with strong magneto-optical terms: B = −Δτ K′ has eigenvalues ±a (dichroism) and ±i b (Faraday rotation), and in the line wings b is large (up to 10⁴ rad in a step) while a is small (median about 10⁻²·⁵). The squarings follow the rotation, about log₂ b of them (4.4 on average, up to 13).

K′ has the structure of a generator of the Lorentz group (η symmetric in the first row and column, ρ antisymmetric in the 3 × 3 block), so a² and −b² are the roots of s² − p s − q², p = Δτ² (η² − ρ²), q = Δτ² η·ρ, and B satisfies (B² − a²)(B² + b²) = 0. Any function of A is then a cubic in B: f(A) = α₀ + β₀ B + α₁ B² + β₁ B³, with the coefficients from the even and odd parts of g(x) = f(−Δτ + x) on the pairs ±a and ±i b. `exp_closed_form` computes them for e^A and φ₁…φ₄, sums them over the five vectors of the step (I_uw and the Hermite coefficients of S) and applies the cubic by Horner's rule, three products with B, so no matrix function is formed. The even and odd parts are computed without dividing by a small a or b (`exp_pair`): k = 0 as e^z cosh r and e^z sinh(r)/r, and k ≥ 1 from them by the recurrence φ_{k+1}(w) = (φ_k(w) − 1/k!)/w written for the pair where Δτ ≥ 3, or from the power series below that, so the common case of small a and large b loses nothing. Where K′ is close to degenerate (|η| = |ρ| with η ⊥ ρ: a² + b² below 10 % of Δτ² (η² + ρ²), where the interpolation's error grows as the inverse of that ratio, measured 8, 17 and 37 ulp at 0.59, 0.07 and 0.02), or not of that form, `exp_phi4` is used as before, and so are the thick steps, which already had a cheap path.

Accuracy, against the same step in long double (prototype, 300 000 random steps binned by a and b): the closed form is as accurate as the Taylor series, 10⁻¹⁶ to 10⁻¹³ relative up to b ≈ 10, growing as b ε beyond, where both are limited by the rounding of the phase b (at b ≈ 10⁵: 2 × 10⁻⁹ against 4 × 10⁻¹⁰ for the Taylor series). `test_stokes_step` compares the two paths (`Stokes_Exp_const_closed`, `Stokes_Exp_const_taylor`, entry points for the test) on a grid of a from 10⁻⁶ to 10² and b from 10⁻⁶ to 10⁴, around Δτ = 3 (where `exp_pair` changes branch) with small a and b, on random K′, and on K′ with |η| = |ρ| just above and below the degeneracy cut; the differences, relative to max(10, b), are 4 × 10⁻¹⁵, 2 × 10⁻¹⁶, 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁴ and 3 × 10⁻¹⁵, within bounds 3 to 5 times larger. The largest are strongly dichroic steps with Δτ of 50 to 70, where both paths are 10⁻¹³ off long double (a limit of the problem, not of either method). It checks that the closed form is taken at every grid point and on the right side of the cut.

Measured (sundog2, one thread, three runs each, interleaved, spread below 0.3 s): the 2-D `FULL_STOKES` case above 28.7 s against 60.0 s on modernize ae1db1e, the same 21 formal solutions and convergence history, emergent spectra equal to 6 × 10⁻¹³ (I, Q, U, V). Per formal solution 1.37 s, as before B-55 (1.36 s).

Results change at rounding level wherever the exponential fallback is taken (polarized multi-D runs, and 1-D `FULL_STOKES` where max |K′| Δτ > 1). `run_tests.sh` in tolerance mode against modernize ae1db1e: passed 38, failed 0; the cases without polarization are byte-identical, the polarized ones (1-D, 2-D and 3-D `FIELD_FREE`, `FULL_STOKES`, `lcbezier`) differ by at most 6.0 × 10⁻¹³ (2-D `FULL_STOKES`, V); peak memory unchanged.
