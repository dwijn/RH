title: Per-transition loops vectorized, -O3 by default (P-7); rowcol covers all periodic-table rows (B-36)

Step 3 of the float plan (§2.4). The loops over depth of the active opacity and the radiative rates (Opacity, addtoRates, addPreconditionedRates; 30–36 % of NLTE runs) did not vectorize: their arrays may alias as far as the compiler can tell, and GCC before 12 does not vectorize at -O2. They now have restrict pointers and are marked RH_SIMD (simd.h, #pragma omp simd with -fopenmp-simd where the compiler takes it), and RelWithDebInfo, the default build type, uses -O3 (option RH_O3). Whole runs are 1.15–1.23× faster with bit-identical results (FAL-C H_6 + Ca II + Mg II PRD 1.16×, moving Mg II hybrid PRD 1.23×, 2-D 32×82 1.15×; one pinned CPU, Xeon E5-2697 v2, gcc 8.5). The loops alone give 1.08–1.11×, -O3 alone 1.07–1.14×. -O3 exposed B-36: rowcol (collision keyword SUMMERS) left the periodic-table row unset for 55 to 86 electrons; it now covers all six rows and stops with an error beyond them.
