title: Chemical equilibrium solved as Newton in the log densities of the free nuclei: converged and positive below 2000 K

`ChemicalEquilibrium` solved the conservation and Saha equations by Newton's method on the densities themselves, from complete dissociation, with no damping, at most 10 steps (`N_MAX_CHEM_ITER`) and a tolerance of 10⁻³ on the relative change; a location that did not converge gave only a warning, and its densities were used. Below about 2000 K, inside the molecules' fit ranges, the first steps overshoot by orders of magnitude and the iteration does not recover in 10 steps: on FAL-C with a cool pocket (`FALC_cool1500`), three locations ended with a relative change of up to 1.4×10³ and negative molecular densities (CH −8×10¹⁵ m⁻³; H2O down to −3×10³² m⁻³ in the 1000 K pocket), and 2n(H₂)/n_H > 1. Inside the map G (`SOLVE_NE = ITERATION`) `ChemicalEquilibriumOfState` did 20 fixed steps of the same iteration.

**The change.** The unknowns are u_i = ln n_i, the logarithms of the densities of the free nuclei only. Every species (free nucleus, H⁻, molecule) is n_s = c_s exp(ν_s · u), so the Saha equations hold exactly and every density is positive. The conservation equations are the gradient of the strictly convex F(u) = Σ_s n_s − Σ_i a_i u_i, whose Hessian Σ_s n_s ν_s ν_sᵀ is positive definite. The equilibrium is its unique minimum. `chemSolve` takes Newton steps on F:
- **Far from the solution** (relative residual above 10⁻³), each step is capped at a change of 30 in any ln n. It is then halved until F decreases enough (Armijo), or doubled while F keeps decreasing. Doubling matters because from the dissociated start at low temperature a Newton step on an exponential shrinks by only about 1 per step.
- **The Hessian** is scaled to unit diagonal and regularized by 10⁻¹², then factored by Cholesky. Far from the solution one species (H2O) can dominate several nuclei, which makes the unregularized Hessian numerically singular.
- **Near the solution**, full Newton steps. Once the residual is below `CHEM_ITER_LIMIT` = 10⁻¹², one more full step ends the iteration, so the result is converged to rounding and G stays a function of its state.
- **Failure**: a location that does not converge in `N_MAX_CHEM_ITER` = 100 steps stops the run, or invalidates a trial point of the solvers (as N-8 and N-40 do).
- **Inside G**, `ChemicalEquilibriumOfState` uses the same solver; `N_CHEM_ITER_OF_STATE` is gone.

Measured before coding with a Python prototype on the inputs dumped from RH (`claude/RH/evidence/t27-n61`):
- FAL-C takes 1–5 steps per location; the cool pockets at 1000 K and 1500 K take at most 15.
- The C implementation agrees with the prototype to 1.5×10⁻¹².
- The old iteration given 300 steps reaches the same solution to 7×10⁻⁸: it then satisfies conservation exactly and the Saha equations to 1.5×10⁻⁷ (its 10⁻³ stopping rule).

**Results.**
- **Warm atmospheres:** the old solution was converged to 10⁻¹⁰ in the Saha equations, so `run_tests.py` changes by at most 1.2×10⁻¹² in I (38/38 pass in tolerance mode).
- **1-D stress set** (21 cases):
  - 19 cases have the same iterations and G evaluations, with differences of 10⁻¹⁴ to 5×10⁻⁷. The largest is `falc_prdng`, which ends oscillating.
  - The two `SOLVE_NE = ITERATION` cases (`falc_solvene`, `shock_h6crd_ne`) take the same iterations and agree to 3×10⁻¹¹.
  - Two cases are sensitive to any perturbation and take a different path: `t5750_h6` (160 iterations before; 217 with gcc, 229 with icx) and `shock_h6` (232 before; 396 with gcc, 208 with icx; `N_MAX_ITER` 500). The gcc and icx builds of the same code differ as much as the old and new code, so these counts measure that sensitivity, not the chemistry.
- **Cool pockets** (FAL-C with a temperature minimum of 700–1500 K):
  - The old code warns 2–3 times per atmosphere and writes negative densities of C2, CN, H2O, CH, O2 and NO.
  - The new code converges everywhere, with no negative density.
  - The emergent spectra change by O(1) at 700–900 K and by up to 10³ at 1000 K (the old negative opacities). For example, I at 1647 nm was between 3×10⁻¹⁰ and 3×10⁻⁸ across the five pockets and is now 3.02×10⁻⁸ in all of them.
  - Below 1000 K the molecules are still absent: their fits end there (N-53).
- **Cost:** wall times unchanged within noise.

**Tests.** New `test_chemequil`:
- Inputs of four locations (1000 K, 1974 K, 5060 K and 10⁵ K) are solved from complete dissociation and compared with the prototype's densities to 10⁻⁹.
- It checks positivity, the conservation residual (below 10⁻¹²), and that the returned densities belong to the returned solution.
- A missing species gets density 0 and the rest still converges.
- Two steps at 1000 K are reported as not converged.
