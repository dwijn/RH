title: The trial-point guard no longer tests continua for inversion

The guard on Anderson and Newton trial points (`guardTrial` in `solver.c`) rejected a point that inverted a transition at a depth where G's own point did not. For a continuum the test was n_i / n*_i > (n_j / n*_j) exp(-hν₀/kT). A diagnostic build printed every violation of each rejected point (S-5 calibration). On `falc_helarge` all of them were continua: high He I levels to the ion at 9–18 kK and He II to He III at 7–8 kK. At 236 of the 246 (continuum, depth) pairs that fired, the converged solution is itself inverted (median ratio 0.69, the same in a plain-G reference converged to 10⁻¹⁰). The test was holding the iteration back from the answer. On `t5750_h6` every guard action was the H n = 4 continuum in the cool upper layers (3.7–3.9 kK), inverted at convergence at 26 of the 28 depths (ratio 0.946); there the test happened to save 3 % of the work. What breaks the formal solver is a negative total opacity (SOLVER.md trap 5). A strong inverted line has made it negative at line centre (H_20's Rydberg lines, S-2). No run with the continuum test off has failed: the S-5 set, and `2d_pert_h6_ca_mg` for 904 iterations. That is evidence, not a bound: an active atom's continua are not part of the background, so an inverted continuum of the dominant absorber could in principle make the total opacity negative. The line test stays. Of the 47 (line, depth) pairs that fired on `falc_mgi66`, 24 are inverted at convergence too, but no case ran better without the line test by enough to give up that protection. The user decided to keep the line test and drop the continuum test. If a case turns up that needs the continuum test, it comes back. Newton's line search uses the same guard, so N-K trial points are affected too.

Errors against the references stay within the S-5 tolerance: largest population error 6.5e-5 (`t5750_h6`, before 5.3e-5), largest spectrum error 4.6e-5 (`t5750_h6`, before 2.9e-5). `shock_h6crd_ne` has no reference and is judged on ‖F_SE‖ (max 3.2e-7 at the end). `run_tests.sh --exact` is byte-identical on all 34 cases: no regression case triggers the continuum test.

2-D, `2d_pert_h6_ca_mg` (Anderson, m = 10): every solver there, plain G included, stalls on a floor of 2–5·10⁻⁵ in the maximum relative change (N-37), so the runs are judged on the first iteration below 10⁻³ and below 10⁻⁴. Before → after: 165 → 171 and 364 → 349 iterations (plain G: 149 and 272). Guard actions in the first 455 iterations go from 138 to 4 (now lines only). On `2d_perf_many` (128 columns, 15 iterations) the old guard rejected every trial point, so Anderson never extrapolated; with the change it extrapolates at 10 of the 15 iterations.
