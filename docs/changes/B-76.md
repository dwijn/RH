title: waveinfo -full lists each wavelength once, five to a row; "in vacuum"

`waveinfo -full` printed rows of five up to `Nlambda - 5` and then the last five wavelengths as its last row, so when their number was not a multiple of 5 the last row repeated up to four of them: 7 wavelengths came out as 1-5 and 3-7, 12 as 1-5, 6-10 and 8-12. Now each comes once, the last row shorter; a multiple of 5 gives the same output as before. The summary line said "in vaccuum". The same in upstream RH (`tools/waveinfo.c:115-122`). The e2e test `waveinfo` checks the listing of 7, 10 and 12 wavelengths. Results unchanged (`run_tests.py --exact`).
