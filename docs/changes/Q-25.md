title: One polarized DELO-Bézier step shared by the 1-D, 2-D and 3-D solvers

`Piece_Stokes_Bezier3_1D`, `_2D` and `_3D` each wrote out the same DELO-Bézier step: the matrices of the control points (with K′², the derivative of K′ and the Bézier coefficients), the right-hand side and the 4 × 4 solve, about 45 lines and an identity matrix per file. They differ only in the derivatives at the upwind point: 1-D carries the centred derivative from the previous point, 2-D and 3-D, whose upwind point is interpolated, use the slope of the quadratic Bézier curve (N-24). The step is now one function, `Bezier3_Stokes_step` (`bezier_aux.c`), that takes the optical path, K′, S and their derivatives at both ends and I at the upwind point, and returns the diagonal of the approximate operator; each solver keeps its own derivatives and calls it. The polarized long characteristics (N-41) use it too. The three solvers shrink by 162 lines, `bezier_aux.c` and `bezier.h` grow by 86.

`run_tests.sh` gets two cases, `2d/fieldfree` and `3d/fieldfree`: the `small` 2-D and 3-D atmospheres with a field that varies across the columns (500 G × (1 + 0.5 sin φ), inclination 45° + 30° sin φ, azimuth φ, with φ = 2π l/Nx in 2-D and 2π(l/Nx + m/Ny) in 3-D), written by `tests/make_field.py` into the work directory when `run_tests.sh` starts; field-free iterations, then polarized final solutions. Before, nothing ran the polarized 2-D and 3-D solvers end to end. With `FULL_STOKES` instead the iteration diverges in 2-D and 3-D, before and after this change (B-55).

The 1-D polarized solution got faster: `1d/fullstokes` takes 2.00 s of CPU time instead of 2.26 s (five alternating runs each, spread below 1 %). `Bezier3_Stokes_step` sits in `bezier_aux.c` with `Bezier3_coeffs` and `m4m`, which the compiler now inlines into it (only `exp` and `solveLinearFast` remain calls), where the 1-D solver called them across files at every depth point.

Results do not change: `run_tests.sh --exact` against the base (the N-39 branch, 683b82c) passes every case (34 checks; the field-free cases, with the new 2-D and 3-D ones, in a second run), the polarized 1-D, 2-D and 3-D ones byte-identical, Q, U and V included.
