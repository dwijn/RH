title: rhsphere retired: spherical symmetry does not fit the shared transfer driver

`rhsphere` is removed: the program, its geometry (`src/geometry/sphere`), its example run (`examples/rhsphere`), the unit test `test_formal_sphere`, the `sph/small` case of `tests/run_tests.py` and its part of the e2e test `solver_default`. Decided 2026-11-24 (`docs/LIBRARY.md` §3.4, §7): it joins the shared transfer driver of Q-42 if it fits cleanly, otherwise it is retired. It does not fit. Its rays are impact-parameter rays whose number of points, μ and weight vary along the ray, and the driver would need six paths for it alone: per-point weights and lengths in J, Γ and the rates; one Feautrier solve per ray instead of one per direction; the emergent intensity from the solver; a negative-τ check over all rays; its own background-scattering sweep and operator row; and refusing the angle-dependent cases it cannot treat (moving atmospheres with lines, polarization, angle-dependent PRD, hydrostatic equilibrium). The evaluation is in `claude/RH/evidence/lib-q43-sphere`.

What only `rhsphere` used goes with it: the `formal` override of the geometry table (`geometryops.h`) and its test in the driver; the per-point angle weights `wk` of `addPreconditionedRates` (`fillgamma.c`; the kernel now has one loop, and `test_coupling` no longer checks them); the mutexes `rate_lock` of lines and continua and `Gamma_lock` of atoms and molecules, which only its formal solution locked (the other geometries accumulate in per-thread copies); and the spherical cases in `readInput`, `initSolution` and `writeSpectrum`.

Kept: the value `SPHERICAL_SYMMETRIC` of `enum Topology`, because `geometry.out` stores the topology as a number and the Python and IDL readers index by it; those readers still read the output of old spherical runs, and the spherical atmospheres `Atmos/FALC_82_s.atmos` and `Atmos/FALXCO_80_s.atmos` stay as data.

Results of 1-D, 2-D and 3-D runs are unchanged (`run_tests.py --exact` against Q-42).
