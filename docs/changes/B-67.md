title: Polarized DELO-Bézier diagonal operator limited to I/S_I: no negative preconditioned upward rates with FULL_STOKES

With `STOKES_MODE = FULL_STOKES` the MALI iterations take the diagonal operator from the polarized DELO-Bézier step (`Bezier3_Stokes_step`, used by the 1-D, 2-D and 3-D short characteristics). It returned the scalar β + θ, which can exceed the weight of S_I at the local point in Stokes I. Then I − Ψη in `addPreconditionedRates` (`fillgamma.c`) is negative, and with it the upward radiative rate of that wavelength and ray. There are two causes.
- **As in B-66.** With K′ = 0 the step is the scalar one, and β + θ exceeds the weight of S_loc where S rises along the ray (C_loc < S_loc). B-66 fixed this in the scalar solvers only.
- **Dichroism.** In thick layers Stokes I has the smaller effective opacity χ_I(1 − |η′|²). So it sees further down the gradient of S and falls below β S_loc + θ C_loc where S rises along the ray. The B-66 Ψ does not cover that. On one step (Δτ = 1 to 30, S_I rising by 2 to 10³ over the step, |η′| = 0.89, no magneto-optical terms), I − (β+θ)S_loc reached −0.49 I and I − Ψ_B-66 S_loc −0.33 I. Magneto-optical terms mostly undo it: with ρ_V = 5 the two are −0.13 I and −0.010 I.

These negative contributions are common. Measured in the `FULL_STOKES` cases of `run_tests.py` (FAL-C, Ca II, 500 G; 1-D on one thread, 2-D and 3-D on eight), run to 10⁻⁶ with a diagnostic build that counts the sign of every per-wavelength, per-ray contribution:

| case | negative upward contributions | negative downward | iterations | with this change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-D | 106 k of 8.3 M | 694 | 22 | 0, 0, 22 |
| 2-D | 643 k of 67 M | 3 226 | 25 | 0, 0, 25 |
| 3-D | 8.1 M of 0.80 G | 31 336 | 28 | 0, 0, 29 |

The summed rates stayed positive, and no rate equation fell back on LU. The negative downward contributions (2hν³/c² + I − Ψη) g − χΨU_j go too: they come from the term in Ψ, which is now smaller there.

`Stokes_psi` (`bezier_aux.c`) limits Ψ to I_loc/S_loc, of Stokes I and S_I at the local point. It returns β + θ where that is no larger, and 0 where I_loc ≤ 0. Ψ is unchanged where S_loc ≤ 0. Both branches of `Bezier3_Stokes_step` (DELO-Bézier and the exponential step of B-55) use it. This bound guarantees I − Ψη ≥ 0 whatever K′ is, as long as the emissivity η of the transition is part of S_I. It holds by construction rather than through the shape of the step. An exact polarized diagonal is the other choice, but it would be larger than β + θ where dichroism raises the weight of S_loc: the larger polarized coefficient (K⁻¹)₀₀ inverted a line in B-55, and the larger exact scalar diagonal converged slower (N-13). Ψ is the preconditioner only, so the converged populations do not change. The scalar solvers keep `Bezier3_psi` (B-66), which already gives I ≥ ΨS there; the bound would change nothing.

Same fixed point, converged to 10⁻⁹:
- **1-D:** 30 iterations on both builds. The spectra agree to 2.7×10⁻⁹ in I and 1×10⁻⁹ in Q, U and V.
- **2-D:** 33 iterations on the base, 34 here. The spectra agree to 1.8×10⁻⁹ in I and 4.3×10⁻⁹ in Q, U and V.
- **At 10⁻⁶:** the spectra agree to 2.2×10⁻⁶ (1-D), 6.4×10⁻⁷ (2-D) and 4.1×10⁻⁶ (3-D), the convergence error.
- **Run time:** 3-D took 140 s against 141 s.

Evidence: `/hao/solar1/dwijn/claude/RH/evidence/t20-b67/` (README; diagnostic patch, run driver, one-step harness and its table, convergence tables, `run_tests` log).

Tests:
- `test_stokes_step`: I_loc ≥ Ψ S_loc on 200 000 random steps. S_I rises by up to 10³ over the step, S and I_uw are in the cone, and K′ is 0 or random and physical (|η′| up to 0.95, with and without ρ). Δτ runs from 10⁻² to 30, covering both the DELO-Bézier and the exponential step. The upwind derivatives are those of 1-D and of 2-D/3-D, plus a one-sided local derivative. With β + θ the check fails by 0.62 of I; Ψ is limited in 18 % of the steps.
- `test_formal_stokes`: I ≥ Ψ S_I at every point of every ray, in both directions, on a slab. The profiles are S_I rising over six decades outward and inward, a jump of 10⁴ at τ = 1, and a random log-uniform profile. K′ is 0, the test's K′, and a dichroic one without ρ (|η′| = 0.94). With β + θ the check fails by up to 0.29 of I with K′ = 0 and 0.54 with the dichroic K′.

Which results change: the iteration path of `STOKES_MODE = FULL_STOKES` runs with an active atom and a polarized transition, in all geometries; the converged solution does not. `NO_STOKES` and `FIELD_FREE` iterations use the scalar solvers and do not change, and neither do final formal solutions, which evaluate no Ψ. `run_tests.py` in tolerance mode, base 16da10e: only `1d/fullstokes` and `2d/fullstokes` differ, by 1.8×10⁻² and 1.5×10⁻² in I at Ca II 866 nm. These cases stop after 4 iterations, so the difference is the iteration path. Every other case is identical to the base (0.0), every thread-count comparison passes, and memory is the same (113 MB).
