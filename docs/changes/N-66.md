title: The background scattering iteration stops on its estimated error, not on its step

The background scattering iteration of N-51 stopped at a wavelength where the largest relative step, max |g − u|/g, was below `BACKGROUND_ITER_LIMIT`. The error left in J is M(I − M)⁻¹ times the step, with M the Jacobian of the step map. That is of order ρ/(1 − ρ) times the step, and ρ reaches 0.999 in the cores of strong scattering lines. On the MURaM column of N-65 with the populations frozen, the emergent intensity was off by 170–400 times the limit at the worst wavelengths, for every limit from 10⁻³ to 10⁻⁸. `ERROR_LIMIT` cannot see that error, because it is a bias of G rather than an error of the outer iteration.

**The change** (`bgscatter.c`).
- **Stopping test.** The iteration stops where F × step ≤ `BACKGROUND_ITER_LIMIT`, with F = min(max(κ, 1), a/(1 − a)).
- **κ.** κ is a secant estimate of ‖(I − M)⁻¹‖. At fixed populations the step map is affine, so the differences of the last 10 evaluated pairs, Δu_i and Δf_i (f = g − u), obey Δf_i = (M − I)Δu_i. 1/κ is then the smallest singular value of M − I on the span of the Δu_i, in the weights of the iteration. It is computed by Gram–Schmidt on the Δu_i, with the same operations applied to the Δf_i, followed by inverse iteration on BᵀB. It is computed only once the step is below the limit.
- **a.** a is the largest albedo. The iteration uses the linear short characteristics (with `S_BEZIER3` too, N-52), where Λσ ≥ 0 and the step is a regular splitting of I − Λσ. So ρ ≤ ‖Λσ‖∞ ≤ a.
- **κ kept per wavelength.** κ is kept per wavelength as the largest so far. From a J close to the solution, the iteration takes a few steps only, and the span of so few differences misses the slowest mode. Without this, a full run at limit 10⁻⁴ still left Mn II 257.68 nm off by 3×10⁻² (below).
- **Skipping a wavelength.** The test that skips a wavelength (the first check of `bgScatterIterate`, and `bgScatterAfter`) uses the same F × step, with the kept κ, or a/(1 − a) before there is one. It replaces the step weighted by albedo.
- **Reporting.** The run log reports the estimated error of the wavelengths that reach `BACKGROUND_N_MAX_ITER`, in place of their step.

**How the estimate was chosen.** Offline, from traces of every background iteration on the frozen full column (18812 wavelengths, each iterated to 10⁻¹²; `stoprules.py`). The table gives the largest error of J divided by the limit T, and the formal solutions at T = 10⁻³.

| stopping rule | T = 10⁻³ | 10⁻⁴ | 10⁻⁵ | 10⁻⁶ | 10⁻⁸ | solves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| step (before) | 171 | 360 | 303 | 402 | 212 | 210k |
| step × ρ/(1 − ρ), ρ from Anderson's residual ratios | 767 | 6550 | 10620 | 59750 | 39190 | 195k |
| step × ρ_G/(1 − ρ_G), the true Jacobi contraction (oracle) | 3.3 | 3.7 | 3.0 | 3.1 | 2.6 | 212k |
| κ × step, κ from 10 pairs | 0.37 | 0.58 | 0.60 | 0.48 | 0.56 | 236k |
| κ × step, κ from 4 pairs (Anderson's history) | 12 | 3.0 | 1.2 | 11 | 1.8 | 228k |

Anderson's residual ratios are smaller than the contraction of the Jacobi map, which is what sets the error, so every rule built on them stops too early.

- **Not fixed: wavelengths whose iteration does not converge.** A few wavelengths do not converge within `BACKGROUND_N_MAX_ITER` (50). In the first sweep of the full column there are three. The worst ends with a step of 0.25 after 50 steps, an estimated error of 37; the other two have 0.4 and 5×10⁻³. Where the run is in a bad state, one of them fails in every sweep, with a step that grows from sweep to sweep. This happened in the run before N-65 and N-66 at limit 10⁻⁶ (from iteration 57, the step growing from 3×10⁻⁶ to 3×10⁻⁴), and in an N-66 run at limit 10⁻³ (from iteration 82). The stricter test does not cause it. It only reports it: the log now gives the estimated error of such a wavelength (up to 0.9) where the step alone looked small. This is N-63 (a), Anderson stagnating where cells are optically thin and the albedo is near 1. The runs at limits 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁵ have 7 and 9 sweeps with one such wavelength, at 1–2.5 times the limit.
- `run_tests.py` against N-65: 38 of 38 pass, every case identical to it at 1 thread (the shipped cases iterate no background wavelength: with `S_BEZIER3` only those with albedo above 0.9 are iterated).

Tests:
- `test_bgscatter`, `check_error_bound`: a scattering slab with ε 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁶, three operators, and limits 10⁻³, 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻⁷. The error of J is now below the limit in all 12 cases. Stopping on the step, 10 of them fail, the worst 41 times the limit.
- `check_warm_start`: from the solution for a 5 % different ε.
- The solve counts recorded for limit 10⁻¹⁰ are updated: 24, 66, 520 and 152, against 23, 36, 494 and 95 before.
