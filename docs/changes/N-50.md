title: Free-free Gaunt factor of hydrogen from the table of van Hoof et al. (2014), not an expansion floored at 1

`Gaunt_ff` (`hydrogen.c`), the Gaunt factor of the hydrogen free-free opacity (`Hydrogen_ff`), was the expansion in (hν/Ry)^1/3 of Seaton (1960), floored at 1. In the far IR and mm the expansion turns negative (at 1 mm: −1.6 at 3000 K, −9.8 at 5000 K, −53 at 10⁴ K), so g was 1 there, where the thermally averaged Gaunt factor is 2.2–3.0 at 1 mm and 2.6–3.5 at 3 mm (3000–10⁴ K); at 100 µm it was 1.0–1.44 against 1.48–2.0. From 400 nm to 10 µm the expansion is within 0.6 % of the table (3000–10⁴ K); at shorter wavelengths it is too high, by 0.6–2 % at 300 nm, 2–5 % at 91 nm and 6–10 % at 23–30 nm (6000–10⁵ K).

`Gaunt_ff` now interpolates the non-relativistic thermally averaged table of van Hoof, Williams, Volk, Chatzikos, Ferland, Lykins, Porter & Wang (2014, MNRAS 444, 420): ⟨g_ff⟩(γ², u), γ² = Z²Ry/kT, u = hν/kT, on 0.2 dex in log γ² = −6…10 and log u = −16…13, relative accuracy 10⁻⁵. The file is `Atoms/gauntff.dat`, the file of http://data.nublado.org/gauntff/ unaltered, with its licence (zlib-style: redistribution allowed with the notice). It is read once in `hydrogenPrepare` (new keyword `GAUNT_FF_DATA`, default `../../Atoms/gauntff.dat`); a malformed file stops the run with a message. ln g is interpolated with four-point Lagrange polynomials in both log γ² and log u (bicubic, one-sided at the edges of the table): positive by construction, and within about 5×10⁻⁵ of the table between its nodes (with every other node left out, the maximum error is 8×10⁻⁴ on the 0.4 dex grid; bilinear in ln g would give about 3×10⁻³ on the full grid). The table covers 1.6×10⁻⁵ K to 1.6×10¹¹ K and every wavelength RH uses; outside it the run stops.

Measured on FAL-C (H_6 active, disk centre; `/hao/solar1/dwijn/claude/RH/evidence/t20-n50/`, README): the brightness temperature rises by 210 K at 0.45 mm, 540 K at 1 mm, 610 K at 1.3 mm and 720 K at 3 mm, with τ = 1 150–380 km higher; nothing changes up to 100 µm.

New unit test `test_gaunt_ff`: the table's values at its nodes (also next to its edges); the classical low-frequency limit (√3/π)[ln((2kT)^3/2/(πe²m^1/2ν)) − 5γ_E/2] to 2×10⁻³ between the nodes for u ≤ 10⁻⁶ and γ² ≥ 20; the mm values; a synthetic table whose ln g is cubic in both variables reproduced to 10⁻¹² between its nodes (also with Z = 2); malformed tables refused. Changing one Lagrange weight fails 1066 of its 1089 checks.

Results: every case of `run_tests.py` changes (tolerance mode against modernize 874da6e; `/hao/solar1/dwijn/claude/RH/evidence/t20-n50/run_tests_bands.txt`). In the plane-parallel cases (1-D, 2-D, 3-D) I changes by at most 2.9×10⁻³ (4.0×10⁻³ in 1d/twoactive), below the Lyman limit; by at most 6×10⁻⁵ at 91–200 nm and 2.5×10⁻⁶ above 200 nm; Q, U, V by at most 2.3×10⁻⁷. The spherical case changes most on the rays that cross only its outer, hot layers: up to 7.4 % at 4 µm and 2 % at 150 nm on limb rays, against 1.4×10⁻⁵ on the central ray. Same code upstream.
