title: BACKGROUND_ITER_LIMIT defaults to 0.1 × the smaller of ITER_LIMIT and ERROR_LIMIT; with NEWTON_KRYLOV at most NEWTON_NOISE

The default limit of the background scattering iteration (N-51) was 0.1 × `ERROR_LIMIT` when that was set, and 0.1 × `ITER_LIMIT` otherwise. Adding `ERROR_LIMIT` 10⁻² to `ITER_LIMIT` 10⁻³, the stricter stopping test, therefore loosened the background solve from 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻³.

**The change.**
- The default is 0.1 × min(`ITER_LIMIT`, `ERROR_LIMIT`), from whichever of the two is set.
- With `SOLVER = NEWTON_KRYLOV` it is at most `NEWTON_NOISE` (10⁻¹⁰ by default). Newton's finite-difference products take h = √`NEWTON_NOISE` and assume G is that smooth. An inner iteration that stops on a limit makes G noisy at the level of the limit. Cut-down MURaM column of N-65 (below), `ITER_LIMIT` 10⁻³, `N_MAX_ITER` 400:
  - Before, the default 10⁻⁴: not converged after 400 G evaluations (largest change 0.24).
  - Now, 10⁻¹⁰: converged after 312 G evaluations, about as long in wall time (18:23 against 18:38).
  - With N-66 on top, 10⁻⁴: not converged after 400. With N-66, 10⁻¹⁰: converged after 253.
  - `e2e_convergence_report` (H₆ + Ca II, 1 thread) needs 34 G evaluations before and 21 now.
- A limit set in the input is used as given.

**Why 0.1.** A sweep of the factor on top of N-66 (stacked on this change), which makes the limit a bound on the error of J. Cut-down MURaM column col_0434_0521 (256.5–259.5 nm, H and Mg active, Kurucz lines with `RLK_SCATTER`, 10 threads), `ERROR_LIMIT` 10⁻², `ITER_LIMIT` 10⁻³, two runs each. Error: largest relative difference of the emergent intensity from a reference run (`ITER_LIMIT` 10⁻⁶, `ERROR_LIMIT` 10⁻⁵, background 10⁻⁹).

The error is set by the outer iteration at every factor. Above 0.1 the stall after the first failed error estimate gets longer (10 iterations at 0.1, 13–18 at 0.3, 16–29 at 1). Below 0.1 the background solves grow by 50–85 % and the iterations do not drop. So 0.1 stays. Before N-66 the same limit left errors of up to 400 times itself, and the factor could not be chosen this way (`claude/RH/evidence/n65-step1`).
