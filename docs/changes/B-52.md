title: 3-D long characteristics of the upward sweep cross the vertical planes on the right side

`setStencil` (`rhsc3d/geometry.c`) tabulated the crossings of a long characteristic with the vertical planes at +(μx, μy)·s from the local point for both winds. The `DOWNWIND` stencils, which `ShortChar` uses upwind of the upward (to-observer) sweep, lie along −(μx, μy)·s, as their `longfracx`, `longfracy` and z fractions already had. Every intermediate point of an upward long characteristic therefore read S and χ at the horizontal mirror image of its position: 0.14 to 0.88 away for dx = 0.016 on a 64 × 64 mesh with layers 0.25 apart, i.e. 9 to 56 columns. The crossings now take the sign of the wind. A horizontally uniform atmosphere hides the error, which is why the slab tests of B-24 did not see it; the emergent intensity (the upward sweep) of every 3-D run with long characteristics in a structured atmosphere was affected.

`test_formal_3d` now checks the upward-sweep long characteristics as well: exact to 5e-16 for the five rays.

On the horizontally varying slab of `test_longchar_3d` (N-39, Bezier short and long characteristics, on top of B-53): with chi = 1 the emergent error drops from 0.25 to 5.9e-2 at N = 8 and from 4.8e-2 to 1.7e-2 at N = 32; with chi = 30 (optically thick sections) I+ was 2.5e-2, 3.4e-2 and 8.9e-2 at N = 8, 16 and 32, growing with refinement, and is now 2.3e-2, 5.0e-3 and 1.4e-3.

Results change in 3-D wherever long characteristics are used. `run_tests.sh` in tolerance mode against the B-53 build (on 1bfaa2d): 33 of 34 cases pass; 3d/small differs by 3.3e-5 in I (uniform atmosphere; see B-53 for why it changes at all). Same code upstream (c95bdf6).
