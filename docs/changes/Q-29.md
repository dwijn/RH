title: setStencil: compact the valid crossings instead of searching a valid[] array (refactor, results unchanged)

Follow-up to B-54 (#384). B-54 was a bug of the `valid[]` bookkeeping in `setStencil` (`rhsc3d/geometry.c`): three places searched or filtered a boolean array over the sorted crossings `vis[]` of the vertical planes (the do-while that finds the first valid crossing for `stencil[0]`, the `if (valid[n])` filter of the fill loop, and the backward search `while (!valid[m]) m--` for the next valid crossing).

Refactor: after the discard pass the valid crossings are compacted in place in `vis[0..Nvalid-1]`, still sorted, and the stencils are built from them with plain indexing: `stencil[0].ds = dz / muz - vis[Nvalid-1].s`, and for the stencil of `vis[n]` (n from Nvalid-1 down to 0) `ds = vis[n].s - vis[n-1].s`, or `vis[0].s` for the last. The `valid[]` array, its allocation and all three searches are gone. The discard rule is unchanged (the same comparison, `<`, so also a NaN distance is treated as before) and stated in the comment: each crossing is compared with its predecessor in the sorted list, also when that predecessor was itself discarded (so a chain of crossings each within `MIN_FRAC` * min(dx, dy) of the previous one is discarded, though the last may be further than that from the last valid one); changing it would change results.

Results unchanged, checked against a build of modernize 2d96f27 (with B-54 merged): `run_tests.sh --exact` passes (all cases, 3-D included, byte-identical at 1 thread); `test_formal_3d` 156 of 156 checks (including the corner rays of B-54 that discard crossings); ctest 37 of 37 on the CI-equivalent and icx builds. The regression suite's 3-D cases discard no crossing, so in addition the FALC 4x4x82 atmosphere with dx = dy = 10, 20 and 40 km (768, 496 and 176 of the long-characteristic stencils lose a crossing, see B-54), 2 iterations, run with both builds: `spectrum.out` and `J.dat` are byte-identical, as are the other output files except `time.out` (timings). The label `changes-results` does not apply.
