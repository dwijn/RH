title: Polarized 2-D and 3-D long characteristics DELO-linear (default) or DELO-Bézier instead of parabolic

`SolveLongStokes` (`rhsc2d/solvelong.c`, `rhsc3d/solvelong.c`) still integrated the source vector along a long characteristic with the parabolic step that N-39 removed from `SolveLong` (DELO-parabolic in S, DELO-linear in K′). Its weight of the downwind S is negative once the upwind section is optically thicker than 2 and about −1/Δτ_dw for a thin downwind section, and the downwind end of the last section is the local point (B-24), so the upwind Stokes vector of the local short characteristic depended negatively on the local S.

`S_INTERPOLATION_LONGCHAR` now applies to the polarized long characteristics too: `S_LINEAR` (default) integrates each section with DELO-linear (`LongChar_Stokes_Linear`: S − K′I linear in τ, the trapezoidal optical path, as `LongChar_Linear`), `S_BEZIER3` with the DELO-Bézier step of the short characteristics (`LongChar_Stokes_Bezier3`, through `Bezier3_Stokes_step` of Q-25, with the Steffen-limited derivatives at the local point and the quadratic Bézier slopes at the upwind point, as `LongChar_Bezier3`). With K′ = 0 they are the unpolarized steps. `SolveLongStokes` is now the polarized counterpart of `SolveLong`, with the same points; the general LU solve with iterative improvement (`SolveLinearEq`, whose singular-matrix error stops the run) and the allocation of a matrix per call go, for the 4 × 4 `solveLinearFast`. `LongChar_Bezier3` and the new step share the Bézier optical path (`longchar_dtau`); `LongChar_Bezier3` is unchanged to the bit (200 000 random sections). The default is linear for the reasons of N-39; with polarization the long characteristics serve mostly the final, non-iterated formal solutions, where accuracy decides, and N-39 found the two schemes equally accurate.

Tests: `test_formal_2d` and `test_formal_3d` run their polarized checks with both schemes (K′ = 0 equal to the unpolarized solver with the same scheme to 6×10⁻¹⁶, Q = U = V = 0; I⁺ exact for linear S and constant K′ to 3×10⁻¹³, on meshes where long characteristics are used). `test_longchar_3d` checks both section steps directly on the sections of N-39's section test: with K′ = 0 equal to the unpolarized steps to 2×10⁻¹⁶ and Q = U = V = 0; with constant χ and random constant K′ (|η| < 1, |ρ| < 2) and linear S, starting from the particular solution, they end on it to rounding (for the Bézier step plus the rounding of the slopes of S over the shorter section, ε/Δτ, which in a thick section has weight ~1).

Results change in 2-D and 3-D wherever polarized long characteristics are used. `run_tests.sh` in tolerance mode against the base (Q-25): all unpolarized cases identical; `2d/fieldfree` differs by up to 1.7×10⁻³ in I, 2.2×10⁻³ in Q, 2.8×10⁻³ in U and 4.6×10⁻² in V, `3d/fieldfree` by 5.7×10⁻⁴, 9.5×10⁻⁴, 2.4×10⁻³ and 1.6×10⁻². The `FULL_STOKES` iteration in 2-D and 3-D diverges with either scheme as it did before (B-55), so N-41 is not its cause. Same code upstream (c95bdf6).
