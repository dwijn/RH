title: PRD no longer diverges on moving realistic atmospheres: the emission profile ratio stays positive (changes results)

The ratio of emission to absorption profile in a PRD line was computed as rho = 1 + gamma (<J>_R - Jbar), gamma = n_i B_ij / (n_j P_j). That form uses the statistical-equilibrium identity n_j P_j = n_i B_ij Jbar + Y_j (Y_j: all other inflow into the upper level, plus n_j Q_elast). During the PRD sub-iterations the populations lag the radiation field, the identity does not hold, and rho goes negative once gamma Jbar > 1. The negative emissivity then gives negative J, negative populations, a negative opacity and a floating-point exception. This is what killed Ca II PRD on the Bifrost cuts. rho is now computed without the identity, as (1 + sum_x g_x <J>_R(x)) / (1 + sum_x g_x Jbar(x)) with g_x = n_i(x) B_ij(x) / Y_j, summed over the subordinate lines x with cross redistribution. This form is positive for any populations and equal to the old one in statistical equilibrium, so converged results are unchanged: on FAL-C, Ca II reaches the same populations to 6e-8 with angle-averaged, hybrid and angle-dependent PRD and with XRD, in 37 iterations instead of 46. Unconverged runs change: the 4-iteration regression cases differ by up to 16% in the PRD line cores. In the regression cases twoactive (Lyalpha PRD) and spherical, the old form stalls at a population change of 1e-5; the new one converges to 1e-8, and the two agree to 2e-5. On the Bifrost slice, hybrid PRD now converges in all 126 1-D columns (120 before) and in 2-D (126 x 89, 38 iterations, 4 min on 16 threads; before: floating-point exception at iteration 11). With angle-averaged PRD, 32 of the 126 columns end in a limit cycle at a change of 3e-3 to 1e-2. That is the early exit of the PRD sub-iterations (N-4): with `PRD_N_MAX_ITER = 10`, `PRD_ITER_LIMIT = 1e-4`, 8 of 8 tested columns converge, against 6 of 8 with the old rho (the other two crash). One column dies from an Ng step that overshoots (N-2). Also present upstream.
