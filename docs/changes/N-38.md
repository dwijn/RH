title: PRD_DAMPING: damped update of the PRD emission profile ratio, default 0.7 (1.0 with SOLVER = NG)

With PRD the plain iteration (`FIXED_POINT`) on `2d_pert_h6_ca_mg` with the Mg II atom from before B-44 ended in a period-2 cycle of Mg II: the change of its populations alternated around 4.26×10⁻⁶ from about iteration 355, and the PRD sub-iteration changes of the h and k lines locked at about 10⁻⁵, while H and Ca II went on converging. The cycle lives in the PRD sub-iterations: an even number of them per iteration, or damping the update of ρ by 0.5, removed it (diagnosis in `claude-tmp/s1/n38`).

New keyword `PRD_DAMPING` (KEYWORD_OPTIONAL, in (0, 1]): after each `PRDScatter`/`PRDAngleScatter`, ρ ← ρ_old + w (ρ − ρ_old), in the sub-iterations inside G (`FIXED_POINT`, `ANDERSON`, `NEWTON_KRYLOV`) and in `Redistribute` (`NG`, before PRD Ng). The `PRD_ITER_LIMIT` test of `NG` and the log use the undamped residual, so that damping does not stop the sub-iterations early. Default 0.7, but 1.0 (no damping) with `SOLVER = NG`, which damping slows (below), so that `NG` runs stay as they were. The example `keyword.input` files document it.

Measured (`claude-tmp/s1/n38/sweep`, README; ITER_LIMIT 10⁻⁶, FS = formal solutions, ratio to w = 1.0 of the same solver):
- The cycle: `FIXED_POINT` with the old Mg II atom, w = 1.0, ends at a Mg II change of 4.2552×10⁻⁶ with PRD sub-iteration changes of 1.8×10⁻⁵; at w = 0.7 the Mg II change is 3.2×10⁻⁷ after 500 iterations and falling, the sub-iteration changes 10⁻⁸.
- `ANDERSON` (the default solver): 1-D, 13 cases, geometric mean of the FS ratio 0.994 at w = 0.7 (0.990 at 0.9, 1.027 at 0.5), all converged. 2-D (current code): `p2_bf12_mg` 0.888 (136 → 119 iterations), `p2_pert` 0.990; 3-D `p3_pert` 1.015.
- **`NG` is slowed by damping**: FS ratio at w = 0.7 1.117 in 1-D (geometric mean), 1.013 and 1.040 in 2-D, 1.287 in 3-D, and more at smaller w (3-D: 1.645 at 0.5). Hence its default of 1.0. With damping set explicitly under `NG`, an accepted PRD Ng step now counts in the `PRD_ITER_LIMIT` test (the larger of the residual and the change after the step).
- `FIXED_POINT`: the PRD sub-iterations are not what limits it (FS unchanged between w = 1.0 and 0.5 in 3-D).
- No run lost convergence through damping at w = 0.7. (An earlier SIGFPE of `p2_bf12_mg` at w = 1.0 was N-39; it converges on the current code.)

Results change wherever PRD is used with the G solvers (`ANDERSON`, the default, `FIXED_POINT`, `NEWTON_KRYLOV`); `NG` runs are unchanged. `run_tests.sh` in tolerance mode against modernize (15bfede), all cases stopped after 4 iterations, so the differences are those of the iteration path, not of the converged solution: unchanged without PRD (`1d/crd`), and a 1-D `SOLVER = NG` run is byte-identical to the base (spectrum, populations, J); the PRD cases differ by up to 2.5×10⁻² in I in 1-D (Ca II K, 393.3 nm), 8.5×10⁻² in `1d/twoactive` (Lyα), 2.4×10⁻² in 2-D, 6.8×10⁻² in 3-D and 6.9×10⁻² in `sph/small`. `e2e_solver_g` now gives its `SOLVER = NG` reference runs `PRD_DAMPING = 0.7`, so that the `FIXED_POINT` runs, with the keyword unset, still reproduce them bit for bit.
