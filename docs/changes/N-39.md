title: 2-D and 3-D long characteristics integrate S linearly (default) or with a cubic Bézier step instead of a parabola

`SolveLong` (`rhsc2d/solvelong.c`, `rhsc3d/solvelong.c`) integrated the source function along a long characteristic with a parabolic step through three points. The weight of the downwind S in that step, (w₂ − w₁Δτ_uw)/(Δτ_dw(Δτ_uw + Δτ_dw)), is negative once the upwind section is optically thicker than 2 and about −1/Δτ_dw for a thin downwind section. Since B-24 (#152) the downwind end of the last section is the local point, so the intensity at the upwind point of the short characteristic, and with it J, depended negatively on the local S. On `3d_pert_h6_ca` the lambda iteration of the background scattering in `initScatter` then alternated with a growing amplitude (relative changes of J up to 8×10⁵), the first iteration changed the populations by 10⁴⁸ and the run died with SIGFPE. Upstream (before B-24) ends in a bounded period-2 cycle on the same input; making only the last section linear reproduces that bounded cycle, so it has the same cause. The fallback to a linear step only where the parabolic I came out negative did not catch this.

Each section is now integrated either with S linear in τ (`LongChar_Linear`) or with the monotonic cubic Bézier step of the short characteristics (`LongChar_Bezier3`, `bezier_aux.c`: a Steffen-limited centred derivative at the local point, χ integrated with the same Bézier curve). In both all weights are non-negative, so I stays between the extremes of the upwind intensity and the source function, however thin or thick the sections are. The new keyword `S_INTERPOLATION_LONGCHAR` selects `S_LINEAR` (default) or `S_BEZIER3`; it applies to the unpolarized 2-D and 3-D formal solutions. With either scheme `3d_pert_h6_ca` converges (`ITER_LIMIT` 10⁻⁶: linear 168 iterations, Bézier 160).

Why linear is the default. The two schemes are equally accurate in every test made, and linear converges more reliably:
- Accuracy, perturbed 2-D FAL-C (`2d_pert_h6_ca_mg`'s atmosphere). Refined horizontally (32, 64, 128 columns, Nz 82), the schemes differ by a 99th percentile of 2–3×10⁻³ in the populations, independent of the column count, while either differs from the 128-column solution by 0.24–1.3. Refined vertically (Nz 82, 163, 325, original heights kept), the schemes' difference falls from 2–3×10⁻³ to 4–7×10⁻⁴ (99th percentile) from Nz 82 to 163. Against Nz 325 both have the same error, to three digits: p99 0.5–1.7 at Nz 82 and 0.3–0.9 at Nz 163. That atmosphere is far from resolved, and the long-characteristic scheme is not what limits its accuracy.
- Accuracy, horizontally uniform 2-D (8 columns) against `rhf1d` at 16 times the vertical resolution: linear and Bézier differ from each other by at most 1.3×10⁻³ at Nz 82, 1.8×10⁻⁴ at 163 and 3×10⁻⁵ at 325, and from the 1-D reference to a p99 of 0.8–1.7×10⁻² (Nz 82), 3×10⁻³ (163) and 1.2×10⁻³ (325), comparable to `rhf1d` on the same grid (1.1–1.9×10⁻², 3–5×10⁻³, 7×10⁻⁴). The column-to-column spread of a uniform atmosphere (the first column of each periodic row takes its upwind intensity from a long characteristic, the others interpolate it) falls 5–7 times per doubling of Nz, for both schemes alike.
- Convergence (Anderson, `ITER_LIMIT` 10⁻⁶): on `2d_pert_h6_ca_mg` linear needs 177 iterations, Bézier 276; on the 32-column perturbed case linear converges in 89 and Bézier stalls at 2.3×10⁻⁶ after 400; at 128 columns both converge (83, 84); at Nz 163 both do (119, 121). At 64 columns neither reaches 10⁻⁶, and neither does at Nz 325 within 400 iterations.
- Cost per iteration: equal within ±10 %.

The 2-D and 3-D example `keyword.input` files document the keyword. `test_longchar_3d` checks both schemes, with linear and Bézier short characteristics and both horizontal interpolations, on a horizontally varying slab with an exact solution (S = s₀ + s₁τ + A sin 2π(x + y)), with optically thin and thick sections: the emergent errors stay below set bounds and fall with refinement, and the two long-characteristic schemes agree.

Results change in 2-D and 3-D wherever long characteristics are used. `run_tests.sh` in tolerance mode against modernize (cc171d0): 32 of 34 cases pass; `2d/small` differs by up to 1.1×10⁻² in I (115 nm), `3d/small` by 2.1×10⁻³ (142 nm). Same code upstream (c95bdf6).
