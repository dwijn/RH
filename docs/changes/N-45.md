title: H-minus free-free opacity from the fits of John (1988) at all wavelengths, theta clamped at 3.6

`Hminus_ff` (`hydrogen.c`) used the table of Stilley & Callaway (1970) below 9113 nm (17 wavelengths from 0 to 9113 nm by 16 values of theta = 5040/T from 0.5 to 2.0, bilinear, theta clamped to [0.5, 2.0], linear from k = 0 at lambda = 0) and the fit of John (1988, A&A 193, 189, Table 3a) to Bell & Berrington (1987, J. Phys. B 20, 801) from 9113 nm on (`Hminus_ff_long`, no clamp). It now uses John's fits at all wavelengths:
- lambda >= 364.5 nm: Table 3a; 182.3 <= lambda < 364.5 nm: Table 3b. They reproduce Bell & Berrington's Table 1 to 0.986-1.015 at theta = 0.5, 1, 2 and 3.6 from 182.3 nm to 15.2 micron. At 364.5 nm 3a/3b = 0.999-1.006 for theta = 0.5-3.6 (largest at theta = 2.6); below theta = 0.5, where both are extrapolated, they part (0.97 at theta = 0.3, 0.81 at 0.1).
- lambda < 182.3 nm: k(182.3 nm) (lambda / 182.3 nm)^2. Neither source goes below 182.3 nm, and Table 3b cannot be extrapolated (it turns negative below 150 nm at theta = 0.5). The tail need not be accurate: H-minus free-free is at most 1 % of the continuum opacity below 182 nm in FAL-C at tau_500 = 1.
- theta > 3.6 (T < 1400 K, the edge of Bell & Berrington) is taken at 3.6: beyond, the fits stop rising with theta (from theta = 4.4 at some wavelengths) and turn negative from theta = 7 (near 400 and 750 nm). This also applies beyond 9113 nm, where `Hminus_ff_long` had no clamp. Below theta = 0.5 the fits are extrapolated, as Table 3a already was beyond 9113 nm; they stay positive and rise with theta down to theta = 0.05 from 50 nm to 1 mm, but their accuracy there is unknown.

Against Bell & Berrington the old table was 0.95-1.16 at theta = 0.5, 0.90-1.07 at theta = 1 and 0.86-1.01 at theta = 2 (worst at 182-304 nm), and below 2520 K it gave the 2520 K values (0.46-0.65 of Bell & Berrington at 1400 K), with a jump of up to +55 % at 9113 nm. `Hminus_ff_long` is folded into `Hminus_ff` and removed; the new `Hminus_ff_kappa(lambda, theta)` returns the coefficient per H atom and unit electron pressure [m^5/J]. The opacity tables are constant, so the theta index table that `hydrogenPrepare` built for `Hminus_ff` is gone.

FAL-C has theta <= 1.12, so the clamp does not act.
