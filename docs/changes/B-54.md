title: 3-D long characteristics: segment lengths ds when a near-corner crossing is discarded

The long characteristic of a 3-D stencil (`setStencil`, `rhsc3d/geometry.c`) is a chain of points where the ray crosses the vertical planes (YZ and XZ) between the local point and the next layer, plus the start on that layer. Two crossings closer than `MIN_FRAC` * min(dx, dy) (0.1 of a column width; the ray passes close to a cell corner) are too close to be useful and the second one is discarded. The stencils of the points that are kept carry `ds`, the path length to the next point toward the local point. The loop that filled them took that next point to be `vis[n - 1]`, the previous crossing in the sorted list, whether or not that one had been discarded: the ds of the stencil before a discarded crossing ended at the discarded crossing's s, and the following stencil started its segment there, so the ds of the two neighbours no longer matched the positions of their interpolation points, each by the gap to the discarded crossing (up to 0.1 min(dx, dy) in s per corner, in both sweeps and for both planes). The sum of all ds was unaffected (the errors cancel pairwise), which is why a check of the total path does not find it.

Fix: the next point is the nearest valid crossing below n (`for (m = n - 1; !valid[m]; m--)`; `valid[0]` is always TRUE, so the loop ends). The stencil count, the discard rule (unchanged: each crossing is compared with its predecessor in the sorted list) and the interpolation points are as before; only `ds` changes, and only for the stencils next to a discarded crossing.

Test: `check_longchar_positions` in `test_formal_3d.c` takes a ray set and now also runs three rays with ux = 0.6, uy = 0.502 (in three octants, x and y swapped in one): the 6th x plane (s = 10 dx) and the 5th y plane (9.96 dx) are 0.04 dx apart, and so are the 12th and 10th, the 18th and 15th. Every stencil's interpolated point must lie at +-(mu) times the cumulative ds (as for the existing rays), for both sweeps and both interpolations; and for all rays the ds must add up to the path dz / muz to the neighbouring layer (new, passes before and after). Before the fix the position check failed for all 12 combinations (3 rays, 2 sweeps, linear and bicubic) with points off by 7.76e-4 = 0.05 dx (dx = 1/64); after it, 128 of 128 checks pass (position errors below 1e-12).

Results: 3-D runs change where a long characteristic loses a crossing to the discard rule, and only then; the interpolation points and their number are the same, only the ds of the neighbours of a discarded crossing change (by the gap to it, below 0.1 min(dx, dy) in path length; the total path is the same). `run_tests.sh` in tolerance mode against the base build (ae1db1e): passed 38, failed 0; the 3-D cases (the FALC 4x4x82 atmosphere, dx = dy = 100 km) are byte-identical to the base at 1 thread, because none of their 800 long-characteristic stencils discards a crossing. To see an effect, the same atmosphere with dx = dy changed to 10, 20 and 40 km (horizontally uniform, 2 iterations; 768 of 2352, 496 of 1904 and 176 of 1472 long-characteristic stencils lose a crossing) gives spectra that differ from the base by 3.5e-5, 2.3e-5 and 3.3e-5 (largest relative difference in I, `compare_spectra.py`). No changes-results case is covered by the regression suite; the label `changes-results` applies.
