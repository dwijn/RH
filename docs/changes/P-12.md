title: Hybrid PRD: the gas-frame interpolation table is built with a bracketing search instead of scans of the whole wavelength grid

`initSolution` (`initial_xdr.c`) builds the table (`spectrum.nc`, `iprdh`, `cprdh`) that adds the intensity of each wavelength, shifted to the gas frame, to the gas-frame mean intensity `Jgas` of its neighbours. For each of the 2·Nrays·Nspect·Nspace entries it scanned the whole wavelength grid for the points between the shifted previous and current wavelength and between the current and next one, twice (counting, then filling): O(Nspect² Nrays Nspace) on one thread. The grid is sorted and has no duplicates (`SortLambda`), so the points each scan selects are a contiguous range of indices. `prdhRanges` finds the two ranges with a binary search (`firstIndex`, the first point above, or at or above, a wavelength), with the same comparisons as the scans, including the constant extrapolation at the two ends of the grid, and both passes loop over the ranges only.

The table is unchanged to the bit. A stand-alone harness running the old scans and the new ranges on the 118 501-point grid of a Bifrost column (four sections of 3000 points: both ends, the middle, Mg II k) with the column's line-of-sight velocities and with 20 times those gives identical `nc`, `iprdh` and `cprdh`; tables dumped from `rhf1d` before and after on a 1-D Bifrost column with Fe I+II and Mg active (58 502 wavelengths, 77 points, 1.4 GB) are identical, and so are the populations, J, `Jgas` and the flux after three iterations on one thread, and on a Mg-only case (6151 wavelengths).

Time to build the table: 3915 s → 23 s (58 502 wavelengths, 77 points, Apple M2 Pro); with 118 501 wavelengths a six-iteration run on 8 threads takes 110 s instead of 5 h (18 172 s), almost all of which was this table. On the 154-point Bifrost columns of the UV synthesis the old code spent about 10 h of a 20–25 h run here on sundog, and a full 496-point column would have spent about two days.

Results do not change. Not changed: the `PRDH_LIMIT_MEM` branch of the 1-D formal solution (`rhf1d/formal.c`), which does without the table and still scans the grid for every ray of every formal solution.
